Screenbomb Catch their real reaction

Support

Screenbomb is a camera for iPhone. As you take someone's picture, it shows them a line or a photo you chose and catches their reaction in ten photos.

How It Works

  1. Type a line or pick a photo.
  2. Point your iPhone Duo at them. They see themselves on the outside screen.
  3. Press the shutter. They get a countdown: 5, 4, 3, 2.
  4. Where 1 should be, your surprise appears.
  5. Ten photos catch what their face does next. Keep the best and the rest are deleted.

Inside the app, the small "?" at the top of the camera screen points out each control.

Questions

Nothing shows on the outside screen
The outside screen is used when your iPhone Duo is open. The label at the top of the camera reads "Outside screen ready" when it can be used. If it reads "They'll watch this screen", press the shutter, turn the phone to face them, and press the shutter on that screen.
Which iPhones does it work on?
Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later. It is made for iPhone Duo. On other iPhones you turn the screen to face them and it works the same way.
Where do my photos go?
When a run ends you see all ten. Tap the ones worth keeping, then tap Keep. Those are saved to your Photos library and the rest are deleted.
It couldn't save to Photos
Tap Open Settings on the message, then Photos, and allow Screenbomb to add photos. Go back and tap Keep again. Your photos stay on screen until they are saved.
The camera is black, or the app asks for the camera
Screenbomb can't work without the camera. Tap Open Settings in the app and turn on Camera.
Can I turn off the countdown?
Yes. Turn off the Countdown switch beside the shutter, and your reveal appears the moment you press it.
Does it record sound?
No. The microphone is never used. A short silent recording is made during each run so the ten photos can be cut from it, and it is deleted straight away.
