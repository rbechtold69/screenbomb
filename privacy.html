Screenbomb Catch their real reaction

Privacy Policy

In effect from November 26, 2026

Screenbomb does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no connection to the internet.

Camera

The camera is used to photograph the reaction. A silent recording is made during each run and deleted as soon as the ten photos have been cut from it. The microphone is never used.

Photos

A photo you choose to show is read only when you pick it. Photos you keep are saved to your own photo library. The rest are deleted.

Sharing

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. A photo leaves your phone only when you share it yourself.

Your Choices

You can turn the camera and saving to Photos on or off for Screenbomb at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Deleting the app removes its settings and any photos it has not yet saved. Photos you kept stay in your photo library.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
